## What It Is

AOD 9604 (Anti-Obesity Drug 9604) — a 16-amino-acid fragment clipped from the tail end of human growth hormone, engineered to preserve only its fat-burning properties while stripping out everything else.

## What It Does

Human growth hormone does a lot of things — builds muscle, burns fat, and also messes with blood sugar. Scientists at Monash University figured out that the fat-burning function lives in a specific region at one end of the growth hormone molecule. AOD 9604 is just that region, with a small stabilizing addition. It locks onto specialized receptors on fat cells (called beta-3 receptors) that trigger the release of stored fat into the bloodstream to be burned as energy, while also shutting down the process by which your body creates new fat stores. Crucially, it does NOT activate the regular growth hormone receptor, so it doesn't interfere with blood sugar or drive muscle growth.

## Potential Benefits

- Targets deep belly fat and fat under the skin for preferential burning
- Avoids the blood sugar problems and other risks associated with full growth hormone therapy
- Research suggests potential cartilage repair and anti-inflammatory properties beyond fat loss
- Has received FDA "Generally Recognized as Safe" status as a food ingredient
- Animal studies showed significant fat mass reduction and increased fat burning
- Phase 2a human trial showed modest weight loss, though a larger follow-up trial did not meet its main goal

## Where It Came From

Discovered at Monash University in Melbourne, Australia, by Frank Ng and colleagues in 1993, during an investigation into which part of growth hormone is responsible for burning fat versus disrupting insulin. Australian biotech company Metabolic Pharmaceuticals then developed the stabilized form and advanced it into human clinical trials in the early 2000s.

## How It's Taken

Here's a breakdown of the available delivery methods and how they compare:

**💉 Injection under the skin / subcutaneous (most effective, most studied)**

Clinical trials testing AOD 9604 for obesity used daily subcutaneous injection. It absorbs reliably and reaches peak plasma levels within 30–60 minutes. This is the standard route for fat metabolism and tissue repair protocols.

**💊 Oral / by mouth (moderate effectiveness, approximately 40% bioavailability in animal models)**

AOD 9604 has demonstrated approximately 40% oral bioavailability in animal studies, which is unusually good for a peptide. Compounded oral capsules and troches are available. Oral doses must be much higher than injectable doses to compensate for losses in the gut (1–10 mg orally vs. 250–500 mcg subcutaneously). Morning dosing on an empty stomach is required for oral forms.

### 💉 Intramuscular / into the muscle (not standard)

Not a typical route for this peptide.

## Dosage Context

- Subcutaneous injection:
- Beginner: 250–300 mcg (micrograms) once daily, injected in the morning on an empty stomach.
- Typical: 300–400 mcg once daily (general fat loss and body recomposition).
- High/advanced: 500 mcg once daily, or 250 mcg twice daily (morning and midday). Clinical obesity trials used up to 30 mg administered daily in divided oral doses, but injectable protocols stay in the 250–500 mcg range.
- Oral form (if using capsules/troches):
- Typical: 1–2 mg per dose once daily on an empty stomach.
- High: Up to 5–10 mg administered daily (as studied in clinical trials).
- AOD 9604 does not significantly raise IGF-1, so it can be more freely dosed without the anabolic concerns of full-sequence growth hormone.

## Timing & Cycling

Morning, on an empty stomach, is the preferred time. This aligns with the body's natural cortisol peak and its natural fat-burning window. Injecting or taking it 30–60 minutes before breakfast (or before fasted morning cardio) maximizes the fat-mobilizing effect. Evening dosing is generally avoided because some users report disrupted sleep. Consistency of timing daily matters more than hitting an exact hour. Pre-workout administration (30–60 minutes before training) is also used to stack the peptide's effect with exercise-driven fat mobilization.

AOD 9604 is cycled. Most protocols run 4–12 weeks, followed by a 2–4 week break. Cycle length varies by goal: athletic cutting phases typically use 4–8 weeks; clinical-style fat loss protocols run 8–12 weeks; cartilage or joint repair protocols run 4–6 weeks. Unlike growth hormone itself, AOD 9604 does not appear to lose effectiveness significantly with continuous use based on available data, but cycling is still the standard practice to allow reassessment and prevent any potential tolerance. After a cycle, body composition and labs are reassessed before deciding on the next phase.

Important: AOD 9604 is not FDA-approved for human use as a research peptide and remains under investigation. All human use is off-label. Physician consultation before use in any form is the standard recommendation across these source materials.
