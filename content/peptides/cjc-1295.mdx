## What It Is

CJC-1295 — a 30-amino-acid lab-engineered version of a natural brain signaling hormone that tells your pituitary gland to release growth hormone. The "DAC" version (Drug Affinity Complex) has been modified to hitch onto a blood protein so it lasts up to 8 days instead of just minutes.

## What It Does

Your hypothalamus (a region of your brain) normally releases a signal hormone every few hours to prompt a pulse of growth hormone from your pituitary. CJC-1295 does the same thing, but far more durably — the DAC version latches onto albumin (the most abundant protein in your blood) through a chemical bond, turning a molecule that would normally break down in minutes into one that circulates for nearly a week. This gives you sustained, pulsing growth hormone release that more closely mimics the natural rhythm rather than one big artificial spike.

## Potential Benefits

- A single injection has been shown in human trials to raise growth hormone levels 2–10 times above baseline for 6+ days
- Could increase IGF-1 (a key growth and repair molecule) by 1.5–3 times for up to 11 days
- May improve body composition — more lean muscle, less body fat
- Could enhance sleep quality and recovery
- Research suggests potential for bone density support
- Works especially powerfully when stacked with ipamorelin, which activates a different growth hormone pathway

## Where It Came From

Developed by ConjuChem Biotechnologies in Montreal, Canada, in the early 2000s using their proprietary "Drug Affinity Complex" technology that extends a peptide's life by bonding it to albumin in the bloodstream. The key human clinical study was published in 2006. Development was halted when the company ran into financial difficulties following a participant death in a Phase 2 trial (judged unrelated to the drug).

## How It's Taken

Here's a breakdown of the available delivery methods and how they compare:

**💉 Injection under the skin / subcutaneous (standard, only practical route)**

CJC-1295 is injected subcutaneously into fatty tissue (abdomen, thigh, upper arm). Clinical trials confirmed that even very small weekly doses raised growth hormone and IGF-1 levels significantly. This is the established route with documented human data.

### 💊 Oral / by mouth (not effective)

Like all peptides, CJC-1295 is destroyed in the digestive system before reaching the bloodstream. No oral form is viable.

### 💉 Intramuscular / into the muscle (not standard)

There is no established protocol for IM injection of CJC-1295. Subcutaneous is preferred.

## Dosage Context

- CJC-1295 comes in two forms: one with a chemical modifier called DAC (Drug Affinity Complex), which makes it last much longer in the body, and one without DAC (also called Mod GRF 1-29). Their dosing differs significantly.
- CJC-1295 WITHOUT DAC (Mod GRF 1-29) — used for pulsed GH release, typically combined with Ipamorelin:
- Beginner: 100 mcg subcutaneously once daily at bedtime.
- Typical: 100–200 mcg per injection, 1–2 times daily.
- High/advanced: 200–300 mcg per injection, 2 times daily.
- CJC-1295 WITH DAC — provides sustained GH elevation:
- Beginner: 1 mg once weekly.
- Typical: 1–2 mg once or twice weekly.
- High/advanced: 2 mg once or twice weekly.
- When combined with Ipamorelin (the most common protocol), the Typical range reported in the literature is 100–200 mcg of each in the same syringe.

## Timing & Cycling

CJC-1295 without DAC: Dosing at the same times as Ipamorelin is common — typically at bedtime (most favored), in the morning fasted, or pre-workout. Administration on an empty stomach (at least 30 minutes before food or 2 hours after eating) is generally called for, since high insulin blunts GH release. Food is generally avoided for 20–30 minutes after dosing.

CJC-1295 with DAC: Once or twice weekly, any time of day, since DAC creates a multi-day sustained release. Timing relative to meals matters less for the DAC version. Five days per week is a common clinical schedule for the combined CJC-1295/Ipamorelin protocol.

CJC-1295 should be cycled for the same reasons as Ipamorelin (its most common pairing): to prevent receptor desensitization and maintain the body's responsiveness to growth hormone signals. The standard cycle is 3 months on / 1 month off, completing 3 cycles per year. Some practitioners use 12–16 week cycles with 4–8 week breaks. Continuous year-round use without breaks risks blunting the pituitary's natural GH response over time. The combination of CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin is typically cycled together as a pair.

Important: CJC-1295 is not FDA-approved for human use as a research peptide and remains under investigation. All human use is off-label. Physician consultation before use in any form is the standard recommendation across these source materials.
