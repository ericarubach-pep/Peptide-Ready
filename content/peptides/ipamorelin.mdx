## What It Is

Ipamorelin — a 5-amino-acid synthetic peptide that mimics a hunger hormone called ghrelin to selectively trigger growth hormone release from the brain's pituitary gland, without the unwanted side effects of older similar drugs.

## What It Does

Your pituitary gland (the master hormone gland in your brain) needs a signal to release growth hormone. Ipamorelin locks onto a specific receptor on pituitary cells (the same one activated by ghrelin, the "hunger hormone") and triggers a burst of growth hormone release. What makes it special compared to older similar drugs is its precision: it stimulates growth hormone without also cranking up cortisol (your stress hormone) or other stress-related hormones. Think of older drugs as a blunt hammer and ipamorelin as a surgical scalpel.

## Potential Benefits

- May increase natural growth hormone release, supporting muscle development and maintenance
- Could accelerate fat burning, particularly through growth hormone's effect on stored fat
- Research suggests it improves sleep quality — specifically the deep, restorative stage of sleep
- May speed up recovery from workouts and injuries
- Could improve bone density, skin elasticity, and mental sharpness via the growth hormone pathway
- Much lower risk of stress hormone spikes compared to older growth hormone-releasing drugs

## Where It Came From

Developed by researchers at Novo Nordisk (Denmark) and first described in 1998 by Kristian Raun and colleagues. It was deliberately engineered to keep all the growth hormone-releasing power of earlier peptides while eliminating the unwanted hormonal side effects — making it the first of its class to achieve that level of precision.

## How It's Taken

Here's a breakdown of the available delivery methods and how they compare:

**💉 Injection under the skin / subcutaneous (standard and most effective)**

Virtually all clinical and research use of Ipamorelin involves subcutaneous injection. The peptide is absorbed into the bloodstream quickly, with peak growth hormone (GH) release occurring within 15–60 minutes. This is the only practical route for reliable effect.

**👃 Intranasal / nasal spray (used in some formulations, less effective)**

Ipamorelin has limited nasal bioavailability. Some compounding pharmacies offer nasal spray versions, but absorption through the nasal lining is significantly lower than injection, meaning much of the dose is lost.

### 💊 Oral / by mouth (not effective)

Ipamorelin is broken down in the stomach and digestive tract before it can work. Oral forms do not produce meaningful GH stimulation.

## Dosage Context

- Lower end of the reported range: 100–150 mcg (micrograms) subcutaneously once daily, typically at bedtime. This gentle initial range is described as allowing an early read on individual response.
- Typical range reported in the literature: 200–300 mcg administered per day, often split into two injections (morning and evening, each 100–150 mcg). This is the most widely used range in both research and clinical wellness protocols. Women tend to respond well at the lower end of this range (100–200 mcg/day); men typically use 200–300 mcg/day.
- Higher end of the range documented in protocols: Up to 400 mcg administered per day, divided into two doses. Going beyond this provides minimal additional GH benefit due to receptor saturation. When stacked with CJC-1295, the combined dose of each peptide is typically 100–200 mcg per injection.

## Timing & Cycling

Timing is important because Ipamorelin mimics the body's natural GH pulses, and GH release is blunted by insulin (released after eating). Commonly cited best times to dose are: (1) First thing in the morning on an empty stomach — supports fat burning and GH pulsatility during low-insulin periods. (2) Before a workout — may enhance muscle repair signals after training. (3) At bedtime — aligns with the body's largest natural GH pulse during deep sleep, ideal for recovery and anti-aging. Dosing at least 30 minutes before a meal, or 2 hours after eating, is standard practice. Eating immediately after dosing is generally avoided — a 20–30 minute wait is typical.

Ipamorelin should be cycled to maintain receptor sensitivity (prevent the receptors from becoming desensitized to repeated stimulation). The most common protocol is 8–12 weeks on, followed by 4 weeks off. Some clinicians use a 12–16 week cycle with a 4–8 week break. A 5-days-on / 2-days-off weekly schedule is sometimes used within a cycle to reduce desensitization risk. Unlike GLP-1 drugs, the benefits of Ipamorelin do not disappear immediately on stopping — effects on body composition and recovery build over the course of a cycle. Continuous use beyond 4–6 months without breaks is generally not recommended.

Important: Ipamorelin is not FDA-approved for human use as a research peptide and remains under investigation. All human use is off-label. Physician consultation before use in any form is the standard recommendation across these source materials.
