## What It Is

Sermorelin (GRF 1-29) — a 29-amino-acid peptide (the first and most active portion of the natural growth hormone-releasing hormone your hypothalamus produces to signal the pituitary gland)

## What It Does

Sermorelin is essentially a shorter, lab-made copy of the signal your brain already sends when it wants the pituitary gland to release growth hormone. When you administer via injection it, it travels to the pituitary and activates the same receptor, triggering a natural, pulsatile release of growth hormone — the same rhythm your body uses naturally. Because it works through your own body's system rather than bypassing it, the normal feedback controls stay intact, preventing dangerously high levels.

## Potential Benefits

- Stimulates the pituitary gland to release growth hormone in a natural, controlled pattern
- May increase lean body mass and reduce visceral (belly) fat
- Could improve bone density with extended use
- Research suggests it can restore growth hormone patterns that decline with age
- May improve skin thickness, sleep quality, and general well-being
- FDA-approved (1997) for diagnosing and treating growth hormone deficiency in children; widely used off-label in adults for anti-aging and body composition goals

## Where It Came From

Identified in the early 1980s in the United States following the isolation of the full growth hormone-releasing hormone from a pancreatic tumor; researchers including Roger Guillemin confirmed the 1–29 fragment had the full biological activity, and the FDA approved it in 1997 under the brand name Geref.

## How It's Taken

Here's a breakdown of the available delivery methods and how they compare:

**💉 Injection under the skin / subcutaneous (only established route for clinical use)**

All FDA-approved use of Sermorelin (previously approved for childhood growth hormone deficiency) and all current compounding clinic protocols use subcutaneous injection. The peptide cannot survive oral digestion. Injected into the belly, thigh, or upper arm with a small insulin-type needle.

**💉 Injection into muscle / intramuscular (sometimes prescribed, similarly effective)**

Some prescribers specify IM injection. Absorption is slightly faster than subcutaneous but the practical difference for most users is minor.

### 💊 Oral / by mouth (not effective)

Sermorelin as a 29-amino acid peptide is completely destroyed by digestive enzymes. No oral form exists.

### 👃 Nasal spray (experimental, not standard)

Some compounding pharmacies have explored nasal-spray Sermorelin, but there is no clinical-trial evidence supporting its use. This route is not part of any established protocol.

## Dosage Context

- Lower end of the reported range: 200–300 mcg administered per day subcutaneously, injected once nightly. This is the common starting point for adults seeking anti-aging or growth hormone support.
- Typical range reported in the literature: 300 mcg per night subcutaneously (approximately 33 units on a standard 0.9 mg/mL concentration syringe). Most once-nightly protocols use 200–500 mcg per injection.
- Higher end of the range documented in protocols: 500 mcg per night is toward the higher end of adult anti-aging use. The original childhood growth-hormone-deficiency clinical protocols used multiple daily doses, but adult anti-aging use typically sticks to a single nightly injection. Body weight does not typically dictate the dose in adult wellness protocols, but the prescribing physician adjusts based on IGF-1 blood levels.

## Timing & Cycling

Sermorelin is almost universally administered once nightly, just before bed. Growth hormone is naturally released in its largest pulse during the first few hours of deep sleep; Sermorelin stimulates the pituitary gland to produce and release GH in sync with this natural rhythm. Injecting before sleep allows Sermorelin to amplify the overnight GH pulse. It should be injected on an empty stomach — eating (especially carbohydrates) in the 1–2 hours before the injection raises insulin, which blunts GH release. No workout-timing recommendation changes the nightly bedtime protocol.

Sermorelin does not carry the same desensitization risk as direct GH secretagogues like Hexarelin. Because Sermorelin works through the body's own natural pituitary feedback loop (it does not bypass it), the pituitary remains responsive to it over time. Some experts argue there is no clinical rationale for cycling Sermorelin at all — it works like a daily physiological signal, similar to how the body's own GHRH functions. However, in practice, most anti-aging clinics prescribe it in 3–6 month courses (often 5 days on, 2 days off per week), followed by a re-evaluation and a break of 1–3 months. Long-term use (6+ months or indefinite) is considered safe under medical supervision, with regular monitoring of IGF-1 and GH levels.

Important: Sermorelin (GRF 1-29) is not FDA-approved for human use as a research peptide and remains under investigation. All human use is off-label. Physician consultation before use in any form is the standard recommendation across these source materials.
