## What It Is

Tesamorelin — a 44-amino-acid lab-made version of a hormone your body already makes to tell the pituitary gland to release growth hormone

## What It Does

Your brain normally sends a signal hormone to your pituitary gland (a pea-sized gland at the base of your skull) telling it to release growth hormone. Tesamorelin is a tweaked, longer-lasting copy of that signal. Once injected, it locks onto the pituitary's receiving stations and triggers a burst of growth hormone, which then tells the liver to produce another growth-promoting molecule called IGF-1. The end result is that your body preferentially burns deep belly fat without touching the fat just under your skin.

## Potential Benefits

- May significantly reduce deep belly fat (the dangerous kind wrapped around your organs)
- Could improve cholesterol and triglyceride (blood fat) levels
- Research suggests it preserves muscle mass while the body burns fat
- May reduce fat buildup in the liver
- Could improve how people feel about their body image

## Where It Came From

Developed by Theratechnologies Inc. in Montreal, Canada, building on the discovery of the natural signaling hormone in the early 1980s. The FDA approved it in November 2010 to treat excess belly fat in HIV patients.

## How It's Taken

Here's a breakdown of the available delivery methods and how they compare:

### 💉 Injection under the skin / subcutaneous (most effective)

This is the only clinically approved and studied route for Tesamorelin. It goes directly into the tissue and from there into the bloodstream with a fast, predictable effect. The FDA-approved brand (Egrifta) is given only this way.

**💊 Oral tablet or troche / by mouth (low effectiveness, limited availability)**

Peptides are made of amino acids that stomach acid and digestive enzymes break apart before they can be absorbed intact. Any oral form of Tesamorelin loses most of its activity during digestion, so much higher doses would be needed and results are inconsistent. Oral/compounded forms exist but are not standard.

### 💉 Intramuscular injection / into the muscle (not standard)

While technically possible, there are no established clinical protocols for IM Tesamorelin, and the subcutaneous route is preferred because it is easier to self-administer and has well-documented absorption characteristics.

## Dosage Context

- Lower end of the reported range: 1 mg injected under the skin once daily. This is also the lower of the two FDA-approved clinical doses and is described as a sensible initial point, allowing the body to adjust.
- Typical range reported in the literature: 1.4–2 mg injected under the skin once daily. The FDA-approved standard for HIV-related fat accumulation is 2 mg/day (original formulation) or 1.4 mg/day (Egrifta SV formulation). In wellness/anti-aging practice, 1–2 mg administered daily is the common range.
- Higher end of the range documented in protocols: 2 mg administered daily is already the top of the approved range; going beyond this is outside clinical guidelines and is not supported by evidence. No established benefit above 2 mg/day has been shown.
- Dose does not typically need adjustment for body weight in adults.

## Timing & Cycling

Once-daily dosing, typically in the evening, is standard — this aligns with the body's natural growth hormone release pattern, which peaks during the night. Administration into the abdominal area (avoiding the belly button or any scarred skin) is standard, with the exact spot rotated each time. The injection can be done at home after proper training. Dosing at the same time each day is used for consistency. Food does not significantly affect absorption, but evening dosing is the standard practice.

Tesamorelin is typically cycled rather than used continuously forever. A common clinical approach is 2–3 months on followed by 1 month off, repeating in cycles through the year (roughly 3–4 cycles per year). This is partly practical — effects on visceral fat need time to develop — and partly precautionary to assess how the body responds. In the HIV lipodystrophy setting it can be used continuously under physician supervision; stopping it usually causes fat to return. For general wellness use, cycling is the norm to prevent any tolerance and allow periodic reassessment.

Important: Tesamorelin is not FDA-approved for human use as a research peptide and remains under investigation. All human use is off-label. Physician consultation before use in any form is the standard recommendation across these source materials.
