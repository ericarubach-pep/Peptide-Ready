## What It Is

SLU-PP-332 (Estrogen-Related Receptor Pan-Agonist 332) — a small synthetic molecule (not technically a peptide) that activates a trio of proteins inside your cells that normally get switched on by aerobic exercise, essentially tricking your body into running the "exercise program" without moving.

## What It Does

Deep inside your cells are three closely related proteins (called ERR-alpha, ERR-beta, and ERR-gamma) that act as master switches for energy metabolism. When you exercise aerobically, these switches get flipped on, causing your cells to burn fat more efficiently, build more mitochondria (your cells' power plants), and shift muscle fibers toward the endurance type. SLU-PP-332 activates all three of these switches at once, turning on the exact same genetic program that aerobic exercise does — including switching on a gene (DDIT4) specifically induced by cardio workouts — without any actual physical activity required.

## Potential Benefits

- Animal studies showed roughly 12% body weight reduction over 28 days and 10 times less fat accumulation versus controls
- Could improve how the body handles blood sugar and respond to insulin
- May increase aerobic endurance and shift muscles toward more fatigue-resistant fiber types
- Research suggests heart-protective effects against the type of heart failure caused by high blood pressure
- Potential treatment for obesity, type 2 diabetes, fatty liver disease (MASH/NASH), and muscle-wasting conditions
- Could benefit people who cannot exercise due to disability, injury, or illness

## Where It Came From

Developed by Thomas Burris and colleagues at Saint Louis University (SLU) School of Medicine in the USA. The foundational work on activating these receptors was published in 2019, and the full exercise-mimicking profile in obese mouse models was published in 2023.

## How It's Taken

Here's a breakdown of the available delivery methods and how they compare:

### 💊 Oral / by mouth (primary studied route)

SLU-PP-332 is a small synthetic molecule (technically classified as a small molecule compound rather than a traditional peptide) that was designed to be orally active. Animal studies used oral administration. This is the most convenient and primary route.

**💉 Injection under the skin / subcutaneous (used in some practical protocols)**

Because oral bioavailability of the compound may have limitations in humans, some practitioners use injectable forms at lower microgram doses for more direct delivery. Practitioners report that even 250–500 mcg injected subcutaneously produces effects similar to larger oral doses.

### 🏥 Intravenous / IV (not standard)

Not part of any established clinical protocol for this compound.

## Dosage Context

- IMPORTANT NOTE: SLU-PP-332 is a pre-clinical research compound. No human clinical trials have been completed with established dosing guidelines. The following reflects early human use patterns from practitioner experience and extrapolation, not approved medical dosing.
- Oral (reported practitioner range): 100–600 mcg (micrograms) daily. One common product formulation is 200 mcg per dose. Some experts suggest splitting into 2–3 small doses spread through the day (e.g., 100–200 mcg pre-workout, 100–200 mcg post-workout or in the evening) to keep signaling rhythmic rather than delivering one large pulse. Animal studies used doses of several mg/kg — scaling to humans is uncertain.
- Subcutaneous injection: 100–500 mcg per dose, 1–2 times daily, based on practitioner reports. Some users report 250 mcg injected once daily is effective.
- There is no well-validated beginner/typical/high framework yet from controlled human trials.

## Timing & Cycling

Based on its mechanism — activating genes related to exercise metabolism and mitochondrial function — pre-workout and post-workout dosing is the most logical approach to amplify the cellular signals already triggered by exercise. If split-dosing, a dose before exercise and another in the early evening is a common approach. SLU-PP-332 works through the parasympathetic nervous system (the 'rest and digest' system) rather than stimulating system, so it does not cause stimulant effects and can be taken without concern for disrupting sleep even if taken in the evening.

Unlike many peptides that stimulate hormonal or pituitary pathways and require cycling to prevent desensitization, SLU-PP-332 works through gene transcription (turning genes on or off) via nuclear receptors, not through receptor systems prone to desensitization. Some practitioners and sources indicate it can be taken continuously without cycling. Its mechanism does not involve feedback loops that would be disrupted by continuous use. However, since human long-term safety data is extremely limited, continuous use should only be considered under medical supervision. This compound is early-stage — its safety profile beyond preclinical animal studies is not yet established in humans.

Important: SLU-PP-332 is not FDA-approved for human use as a research peptide and remains under investigation. All human use is off-label. Physician consultation before use in any form is the standard recommendation across these source materials.
