## What It Is

Semax — a 7-amino-acid synthetic peptide (a modified fragment of a stress-response hormone, engineered to last much longer and act on the brain without the hormone's other side effects)

## What It Does

Semax quickly raises levels of a brain growth factor called BDNF (think of it as fertilizer for neurons) in the hippocampus, the part of your brain responsible for learning and memory. It also activates the brain's mood-regulating systems (serotonin and dopamine pathways) and slows the breakdown of the brain's own natural regulating peptides, letting them work longer. After a stroke or reduced blood flow to the brain, it helps dampen the inflammatory and toxic cascade that destroys neurons.

## Potential Benefits

- Approved in Russia for treatment of stroke and mini-strokes (brief episodes of reduced blood flow to the brain)
- May enhance focus, memory, and cognitive performance
- Research suggests neuroprotective effects against low oxygen and toxic overstimulation of neurons
- Could have antidepressant and anti-anxiety effects
- May help regulate the immune system
- Research suggests potential benefit in ADHD, eye nerve damage, and neurodegenerative conditions
- Listed on Russia's national essential medicines list

## Where It Came From

Developed at the Institute of Molecular Genetics in Moscow, Russia, by a team led by N.F. Myasoedov; first synthesized in 1982, published in the scientific literature in 1991, and its name translates from Russian as 'seven amino acids.'

## How It's Taken

Here's a breakdown of the available delivery methods and how they compare:

**💉 Nasal spray / intranasal (most effective for cognitive and neuroprotective effects**

actually superior to injection for brain targeting) — Semax was developed and approved in Russia specifically as a nasal spray, and unusually for a peptide, research shows it may be more effective intranasally than via injection for nootropic effects. The peptide travels along the olfactory nerve pathway from the nasal passage directly into the brain, bypassing the blood-brain barrier. Effects are felt quickly, often within 15–30 minutes.

**💉 Injection under the skin / subcutaneous (also effective, preferred for systemic or non-brain effects)**

Subcutaneous injection delivers Semax into the bloodstream and is used when systemic effects are desired or when nasal spray is not available. Slightly less direct for brain targeting.

### 💊 Oral / by mouth (not established, not recommended)

No validated oral protocols for Semax exist. As a 7-amino acid peptide, it is expected to be broken down by digestion.

## Dosage Context

- Lower end of the reported range: 100–200 mcg administered per day (nasal spray, split into 1–2 daily doses). This corresponds to 1–2 drops or sprays of a standard 0.1% Semax solution per nostril.
- Typical range reported in the literature: 300–600 mcg administered per day, divided into 2 doses (morning and early afternoon). Research references commonly cite a range of 0.015–0.050 mg/kg for intranasal use — for a 70 kg person, that is roughly 1,050–3,500 mcg administered per day, though most practical protocols use the lower end of this range (300–600 mcg).
- Higher end of the range documented in protocols: Up to 900–1,000 mcg administered per day subcutaneously or intranasally, used in specific neurological treatment contexts in Russia. Doses above 1,000 mcg administered per day are not common in community or clinic protocols and lack broad safety data. Body weight does influence the research-based dose ranges.

## Timing & Cycling

Morning is the primary dosing time for Semax, as it supports focus, memory, and mental performance during active hours. A second dose can be taken in the early afternoon if desired, but avoid late-afternoon or evening doses — Semax is stimulating in nature and can cause sleep disruption. Cycle length matters: Russian clinical protocols use courses of 10–20 days followed by a break of 1–2 weeks. Semax does not need to be taken with food. No workout-specific timing is established.

Semax is cycled, not taken indefinitely. The standard protocol derived from Russian clinical use is 10–20 days on, then 1–2 weeks off, with repeated courses as needed (typically 2–4 times per year). Some biohacking protocols use slightly longer cycles of 4–6 weeks with a 1–2 week break. Continuous daily use year-round is not recommended because the neuropeptide's effects on BDNF (a brain growth factor) and cognitive enhancement appear to be best maintained with periodic breaks that allow the brain's receptor systems to remain sensitive. It is not a drug designed for indefinite daily administration.

Important: Semax (MEHFPGP) is not FDA-approved for human use as a research peptide and remains under investigation. All human use is off-label. Physician consultation before use in any form is the standard recommendation across these source materials.
